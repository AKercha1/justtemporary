# Plan

## Planned Steps
1.  **Brainstorming Session:** Conduct a brainstorming session to generate initial ideas for the art project. This will involve exploring various themes, concepts, and potential artistic mediums.
2.  **Concept Development:** Select a promising concept from the brainstorming session and develop it further. This includes defining the project's core message or intent.
3.  **Preliminary Design:** Create preliminary sketches, mood boards, or digital mock-ups to visualize the chosen concept.
4.  **Material and Technique Research:** Research potential materials, tools, and artistic techniques that could be used to realize the project.
5.  **Feedback Gathering (Optional):** Seek initial feedback on the developed concept and design from peers or mentors.

## Open Questions and Assumptions
*   **What is the primary artistic medium for this project?** (e.g., painting, sculpture, digital art, performance art)
*   **What is the core theme or message the art project aims to convey?**
*   **Who is the intended audience for this art project?**
*   **What are the available resources (e.g., budget, time, space, collaborators) for the project?**
*   **Assumption:** There is a desire to create something novel and artistically meaningful.
*   **Assumption:** The project will eventually move beyond conceptualization into a creation phase, though that is out of scope for this initial plan.
